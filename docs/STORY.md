# Whale Road: story bible

Owner: story. Data lives in `js/data/heroes.js`, `js/data/lore.js` and `js/data/quests/`
(`sagas.js` gathers `saga_<hero>.js`; `side.js`; `items.js`; `lines.js` holds the cast and the
small builders). `node tests/quests.test.mjs` checks all of it against the contract.

Everything here is newly written for this game. Places, gods and peoples are historical;
every speaking character is invented. Real rulers of the time (the Emperor in Miklagarðr, the
princess of Kænugarðr) are only ever mentioned in passing and never speak.

## The world, c. 950

Spring of the year 950. The Whale Road is every sea and river road a ship can take: the fjords
of Norway, the North Sea to Jórvík, Dyflin and the Frankish coast, the long crossing to Færeyjar
and Ísland, the Baltic with its amber shore and island hoards, and the river road through
Aldeigjuborg, Hólmgarðr and Kænugarðr down the rapids to Miklagarðr, the Great City.

Trade is the heart of it: stockfish and wadmal from the north, salt and wine from the west,
iron and furs from the Svear and the Slavs, amber from the Aesti, silk from the Greeks. Jarls,
reeves, princes, elders and imperial clerks rule the harbours and open them to captains who help.

Tone: warm, witty, all ages. Battles are against raiders, collectors, sea beasts and the restless
drowned; nobody is hurt in a way that is described. No slave trade, no gore, no romance plots.
Every culture is shown through people who are clever, funny and decent on their own terms.

## The threat that ties the sagas: the Tallyman and Rán's knot

**Svartkell the Tallyman** was a coin-weigher in Birka, and long before that the purser of the
sea-king Ketill Grey-Keel. Nine winters ago Birka caught him shaving silver off every coin he
weighed ("rounding", he called it) and banished him. Ísland's Althing outlawed him too. He swore
he would own the sea, and jumped into it.

He came to Rán's hall under the waves, where the sea goddess keeps the drowned in her net. He
challenged her to a game of counting waves, cheated, and while she counted he cut **one knot
from her net** and swam up with it. Since then the sea has been one wave short: every ninth wave
is missing, and those who notice (a monk, a sheep-reeve, a Sámi trader, a völva) are worried.

With the knot, Svartkell commands what the net once held. Drowned crews row his **draugr
ships** without wind and lift old hoards off the seabed. He pays in that drowned silver, which
stays cold in the sun, and he uses it to buy the debts of captains all along the Whale Road
through his **Grey Company**. Every debtor is a ship he can call on. His hired muscle are the
Jómsviking captains of **Jómsborg**, whose gate he holds. The torn net lets other things loose:
**storm crows** on the cliffs, a **sea serpent** in the firths, and, at the end, Rán's own
watchdog, the **kraken**.

He is not a monster: he is a vain, tidy, furious accountant who loves straight columns and hates
anything done for free. Every saga ends with him losing the knot back to the sea, and with Rán
setting him to count her waves forever. No one dies.

**How the sagas relate.** Each saga is its own telling, with its hero at the centre and the same
cast around them. The heroes meet the Tallyman from different sides: Ylva through his debts,
Bjarki through his fortress, Sæunn through the broken net, Hrafn through his story, Ketill
through their shared past, Gríma through the favours he cannot stand. Small cross-references
(old Orm's hoard lifted from Ravnfjord fjord, Hrafn's unpaid harp, Kolli in Hegvald's barn) appear
in more than one saga as rumours and asides.

## Heroes and their arcs

### Ylva Steadyhand (start: Ravnfjord, knarr, 1 200 s, debt 3 000 s)
A fisherman's widow who pays her husband's debt and then frees every captain on the Whale Road.
1. Learns the trade: Ravnfjord stockfish to Kaupang (tutorial).
2. Pays the first 1 000 s to Bárðr Pennyclaw; fetches Orm's crate from Hálogaland: walrus ivory
   and a note ("pay what I owe and not a coin more").
3. Heiðabýr: Orm's note was sold to a grey-cloaked buyer who orders water in mead halls.
4. Birka: the buyer is Svartkell; a ledger page from a Gotland hoard shows he collects captains.
5. Kaupang and Truso: out-trade him; grow to 15 000 s.
6. Jórvík: pays off the comb-makers' notes, beats two collector ships, wins a busse.
7. Birka's guild and Hegvald's letters open the river road.
8. Hólmgarðr: storms Jómsborg; finds Svartkell's strongbox key.
9. Aldeigjuborg: clears the Gulf; grows to 60 000 s.
10. Miklagarðr: pays the fees, opens his strongroom, takes four hundred debt notes. Orkney points
    to "the edge of the world".
11. Ísland: burns the notes before the Lawspeaker, grows to 150 000 s. **Finale** in the Iceland
    Sea, fog: raider_drakkar (Svartkell), 2 collector_snekkja, draugr_ship.

### Bjarki Bear-Shoulder (start: Heiðabýr, karvi, 1 200 s)
A huge, gentle ex-Jómsviking who gathers twenty harbours into one alliance.
1. Auðun's timber and a Kattegat skirmish (tutorial). 2. Truso's amber shore. 3. Finds his old
oar-mate Kolli in Hegvald's barn and learns of the Tallyman. 4. Kaupang's spears and Radoslav's
friendship. 5. Wrestles Jarl Hrolleif, gently; wins a snekkja. 6. Beats a Jómsviking skeid.
7. The Western Oath: Jórvík and Hamwic swear together. 8. The river princes. 9. Kænugarðr and
the pilot Svitozar. 10. Miklagarðr's forms; breaks the rapids blockade; Orkney joins.
11. Borrows Ísland's Sun Banner. **Finale** at Jómsborg (fortress): fortress_gate,
2 fortress_tower, jomsviking_skeid, raider_drakkar.

### Sæunn Mist-Eye (start: Hlaðir, karvi, 1 500 s)
A young völva who mends Rán's net with a new knot made by many hands.
1. Heiðrún and the grey ship in Ravnfjord fjord. 2. Áilu the Sámi trader hears the missing wave.
3. Orkney's storm crows. 4. Brother Colm's tide-book; Gudrið's sheep noticed first. 5. Cold coins
of drowned silver, read by Jórvík's moneyers. 6. Lays a draugr ship to rest; wins a byrding.
7. Truso's amber spindle. 8. Hólmgarðr's guild thread; storms Jómsborg. 9. South along the river.
10. Magistra Eudokia's sailors' tale; Greek silk; Gudrið spins the woven knot in Færeyjar.
11. Blesses the knot at Ísland's altar-rock. **Finale** in the Iceland Sea, storm: kraken,
2 storm_crow, serpent.

### Hrafn Half-Rhyme (start: Jórvík, knarr, 800 s, debt 2 000 s)
A skald who never finishes a verse chases the Tallyman's story to its end.
1. Osgar's feast and a first payment on the harp. 2. A half-verse for the Viscount of Rúðuborg.
3. The riddle-stick left in Hamwic. 4. Védís reads it: a ledger, signed Svartkell. 5. Ása's grey
ships; wins a busse. 6. Orkney's crows. 7. Ísland's verdict on the outlaw. 8. Taunts Jómsborg's gate
open. 9. Svartkell's tally-book under Birka's floor: "Rán. Lost to me at counting waves."
10. The river song with no ending. 11. Eudokia's last verse. **Finale** in the North Sea, fog:
3 draugr_ship and a storm_crow, sung home.

### Ketill Grey-Keel (unlockable; start: Birka, snekkja, 6 000 s)
An old sea-king settles the account of the purser he never checked.
1. Recognises the tally-marks. 2. Truso's amber shore again (and a broken chair). 3. Zlata's furs.
4. Hólmgarðr's council votes, after one fistfight. 5. The Gulf blockade; wins a skeid.
6. Miklagarðr: Svartkell borrowed against a goddess's knot. 7. Collectors in the Baltic.
8. Storms Jómsborg. 9. Hlaðir, where he hired Svartkell. 10. Ísland names him champion; a sunstone.
11. **Finale** in the Skagerrak, storm: raider_drakkar, 2 draugr_ship, serpent, collector_snekkja.

### Gríma Knotgrip (unlockable; start: Hálogaland, byrding, 2 500 s; all ports but Jómsborg open)
Half troll, all heart. Her saga is the optional tasks.
1. Granny Gnúpa's bargain: every errand on the Whale Road. 2. Frees Jómsborg (renown 15).
3. Requires every side task. **Finale** in the Norwegian Sea, storm: raider_drakkar,
2 collector_snekkja, draugr_ship, storm_crow. She sets Svartkell's ship down gently.

### Custom hero
Name and look chosen by the player; Ylva's talent. It has no saga of its own: it is a face for
Free Trade, or (lead's call) it can play with Ylva's start sets and no chapters.

## Unlock order

Every saga starts with four ports and the six everyday goods (stockfish, salt, grain, timber,
wadmal, honey). A port opens after a task for the ruler of the neighbouring region. Jómsborg is a
fortress until a saga's storming chapter; it opens with hacksilver. The test simulates each saga
and checks that every port and good opens, that every step's port is already open, that some
open market sells every good a chapter asks for, and that every sea battle lies on a route between
open ports.

## Conventions for the engine and the UI

- **Dialogue lines** are `{who:'npc'|'hero', text}`; an npc line may add `npc` (portrait id) and
  `name` when the speaker is not the quest giver (done lines after a battle, `say` lines at
  another building). Fall back to `giver.npc` / `npcName`.
- **INTRO / ENDINGS** lines also use `who:'narrator'` (no portrait), and the custom hero's lines
  contain `{name}`.
- **Reward flags**: `saga_done_<hero>` on each finale; `guild_<port>` after a guild donation task;
  `sells_<port>_<good>` means that port's market sells that good from now on (side tasks
  side_halo_bell, side_rud_barrels, side_truso_storm). Jómsborg opens through
  `unlockPorts:['jomsborg']` or the fortress win; the sim's own flag is `jomsborg_free`.
- **Side tasks** have `saga:null, chapter:null`. Guild tasks come in pairs: a donate task, then a
  task that `requires` it and turns goods into a charm or quest item.
- **Lore tokens**: `{good}`, `{port}`, `{price}`, `{dir}` (cheap/dear), `{name}`.
