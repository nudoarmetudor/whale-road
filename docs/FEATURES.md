# Feature checklist

Every gameplay system found in the two genre classics Tudor named (a 2005 Caribbean trading
game and a 2009 ancient-Greek one by the same studio), and how Whale Road covers it.
Mechanics are matched; names, story, art, music and layout are our own.

Sources read on 2026-11-25: the 2005 game's official help pages, store pages, and reviews
and player guides on Pocket Gamer, JayIsGames, MyGamer, Gamezebo, GameYum and a Steam
community guide. The fan wiki for both games refused automated access, so a few numbers
(exact ship stats in the 2009 game, crew rules) were not available; where a source was
silent, the design choice is ours and marked *(ours)*.

Legend: `[ ]` not built, `[~]` partly, `[x]` built and tested on phone.
Source tags: **A** = 2005 game, **B** = 2009 game, **AB** = both.

## Modes, heroes, goals
- [ ] Saga mode: story, ports and goods unlock as you go (AB)
- [ ] Free Trade mode: all ports and goods open, no tasks, endless (AB)
- [ ] Free Trade unlocked from the start *(A had it at start, B after finishing; we open it at start)*
- [ ] Four starting heroes, two more unlocked by finishing any saga (B: 4+2; A: 4+1)
- [ ] Custom hero (name, look) unlocked by finishing a saga (A)
- [ ] Each hero has an 11-chapter main saga (B)
- [ ] Shared optional tasks, same for every hero (B)
- [ ] One unlockable hero whose saga is "finish every optional task" (B)
- [ ] Each hero has a combat talent: bar fills as the fleet deals damage, button fires it (B)
- [ ] Score = net worth = silver carried + bank deposits + goods + ships − debt (A)
- [ ] Saga ends with a final boss battle (A) and an ending scene
- [ ] Easy mode toggle at any time: "?" markers on next building and on the sea (B)
- [ ] Ranks that grow with renown *(B mentions "rank"; thresholds ours)*
- [ ] Feats (achievements) per profile, shared by heroes in that profile (B)
- [ ] Several player profiles, each with its own journal and feats (B)
- [ ] Free Trade high-score table *(ours)*

## Map, travel, time
- [ ] One sea chart; tap a discovered port to sail (AB)
- [ ] Locked ports shown with "?" until unlocked by a jarl's tasks (A)
- [ ] Tap/hover a port: services, goods, last prices, travel time (B)
- [ ] Voyage length depends on distance and fleet speed (slowest ship) (AB)
- [ ] Set Sail disabled when the fleet is overloaded (A)
- [ ] Sailing animation along real sea and river routes; days pass (AB)
- [ ] Sailing speed option and in-game fast toggle (A)
- [ ] Storms on voyages: unavoidable hull damage unless warded (A)
- [ ] Rain/fog in battle reduces accuracy/visibility (B)
- [ ] Main-quest markers gold, optional markers blue, on the chart and in town (B)
- [ ] Calendar with seasons; prices vary by time of year (A); winter seas rougher *(ours)*

## Ports and buildings
- [ ] 20 ports, each with a distinct painted scene (A had 20)
- [ ] 3 to 8 buildings per port, varying by town (A)
- [ ] City view: tap buildings to enter (AB); building chips for phones *(ours)*
- [ ] Market (AB)
- [ ] Jarl's hall: quest giver, unlocks ports (A governor, B acropolis)
- [ ] Mead hall: rumours, price tips, contacts, reputation, side tasks (A alehouse)
- [ ] Shipyard: repair, buy/sell ships with trade-in, one ship for sale per visit, armaments +/−, special ammo, fleet order (AB)
- [ ] Altar: sacrifice goods for blessings, quantity or Max (B)
- [ ] Rune-carver: magic charms, some cursed (A alehouse items, B reliquary)
- [ ] Craft guild: donate, then combination tasks that give a free crafted good/item (B)
- [ ] Bank: 10%/year, money reachable only in that town, cannot be robbed (A)
- [ ] Moneylender: up to 2× net worth at 10%/month; overdue → collectors attack, no new loans (AB)
- [ ] Warehouse: store goods in that port (A)
- [ ] Talk button: townsfolk lines, advances quests (B)
- [ ] Left/top panel: silver carried, bank, debt, date, cargo meter (AB)

## Goods and economy
- [ ] 14 goods with min–max price ranges per good (A)
- [ ] Prices differ by port (produced = cheap, demanded = dear) (AB)
- [ ] Prices move over time; refresh at least weekly (A)
- [ ] Seasonal price swings (A)
- [ ] Your trades move the price (buying raises, selling lowers) *(ours, standard genre)*
- [ ] New goods unlock through the saga (AB); top good only after a raider fortress falls (A)
- [ ] A town starts selling a good after you sell it 10 units (B)
- [ ] Contraband: not listed; "sell all" only, high price, risk of confiscation and fine (A)
- [ ] Buy screen: amount, Max (cargo room and silver), Reset, running total (B)
- [ ] Sell screen: amount, Max, Sell All (B); shows average price paid *(ours)*
- [ ] Quest goods move to a separate list, not cargo (B)
- [ ] No penalty for reselling at the same port (B)

## Ships, fleet, armaments
- [ ] 8 ship types, faering to drakkar, with hull, cargo, slots, speed, ram, price (A had 7)
- [ ] Fleet of up to 4 ships (B); single-ship start (A)
- [ ] Each armament takes 5 cargo (A)
- [ ] Short and long range, standard and premium armaments (B)
- [ ] Sell armament for immediate refund (B)
- [ ] Special ammo: heavy single, light all, heavy all (A)
- [ ] Repairs cost silver at shipyards (AB)
- [ ] Selling a ship returns its value with armaments; cannot sell if cargo would overflow (B)
- [ ] Fleet screen: drag to reorder; order matters in battle (B)
- [ ] Magic items: +10% cargo, faster reload, +20% hull, storm ward, cheaper blessings (AB)

## Combat
- [ ] Real-time battle that starts paused; one button to start/pause (B)
- [ ] Your fleet on one side, enemies on the other, health bars (AB)
- [ ] Weapons fire automatically after reloading (A)
- [ ] Tap an enemy to focus fire; ships without orders shoot the one in front (AB)
- [ ] Ramming button; cannot ram flying or swimming foes; ramming bigger ships hurts you (B)
- [ ] Ships shuffle to the middle as gaps open; tough ships best in the middle (B)
- [ ] Flee button, not guaranteed, costs damage, no loot (AB)
- [ ] Five blessing buttons with cooldowns, usable only while enemies remain (B)
- [ ] Special-ammo buttons (A)
- [ ] Hero talent bar (B)
- [ ] Loot: silver and dropped goods; needs free cargo (B)
- [ ] Enemy kinds: raiders, warships, sea beasts, flying beasts, ghost ship, fortress (AB)
- [ ] Attack a raider-held fortress port; freeing it opens the port and its goods (A)
- [ ] Moneylender's collectors attack if a loan is overdue (B)
- [ ] Gods send beasts after you when angered *(B; ours: if you skip an altar task)*
- [ ] Battle speed toggle *(reviewers asked for it)*

## Quests
- [ ] Jarl tasks: deliver goods, bring items, visit, hunt raiders, gather across towns (AB)
- [ ] Rescue / retake / cure style multi-step tasks (B)
- [ ] Multi-port tasks later in the saga (A)
- [ ] A set number of tasks for a jarl unlocks the next port (A)
- [ ] Rewards: silver, items, goods unlocks, blessings, ships (AB)
- [ ] Task list that tracks automatically; journal with list + details (AB)
- [ ] Some optional time limits *(ours)*

## UI and system
- [ ] Main menu: Saga, Free Trade, Continue, Load, Options, Help, Credits, Hide menu (watch ships) (A)
- [ ] In-game menu: Resume, Save, Load, Options, Quit to menu (A)
- [ ] Autosave on entering each port; manual save slots with timestamps (B)
- [ ] Options: music volume, effects volume, fullscreen, graphic detail, sailing speed, dialogue speed, tutorial on/off, Easy mode (AB)
- [ ] Hotkeys on desktop: I items, S ship, C cargo, T tasks, L log, F fullscreen, H tutorial (A)
- [ ] Tutorial hints in the first port (A)
- [ ] Ship's log of events (A)
- [ ] Phone: landscape and portrait, touch only *(ours)*
- [ ] Installable as a home-screen app (PWA) and works offline *(ours)*

## Presentation
- [ ] Painted-style port scenes, a distinct look per culture and per port (B)
- [ ] Animated details: water, smoke, birds, flags (A)
- [ ] Hero and NPC portraits in dialogue (AB)
- [ ] Original music, a theme per region plus sea, storm, battle, tavern (A changed music by town and battle)
- [ ] Sound effects for trade, battle, sailing
- [ ] Light humour in dialogue (AB)
